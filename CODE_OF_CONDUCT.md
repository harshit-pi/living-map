# Code of conduct

Living Map follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@harshit-pi](https://github.com/harshit-pi), privately rather than in a public thread. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who do not follow this code.
